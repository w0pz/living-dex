Shiny History

Shinies are listed by date. Use “+ Add shiny” for shinies caught before you used the tracker, or “Edit” to fix an entry. Method Shiny Rate is the estimated chance for the selected method at that encounter; Actual Shiny Rate is one shiny divided by the total encounters through the successful encounter. Phase entries are unexpected shinies found during another hunt. Luck compares each shiny with its odds: “odds by then” is the chance of finding a shiny within that many encounters, so anything under 50% means you beat the odds.
